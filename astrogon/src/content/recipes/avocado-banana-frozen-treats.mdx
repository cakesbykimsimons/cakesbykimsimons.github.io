---
title: Avocado-Banana Frozen Treats
description: A refreshing frozen dessert blending creamy avocado and sweet banana, enriched with coconut cream and maple syrup, then dipped in a crisp dark chocolate shell.
image: "@assets/recipes/avocado-banana-frozen-treats/avocado-banana-frozen-treats.jpg"
imageAlt: "Two green avocado-banana frozen treats on sticks arranged on a plate with one split in half"
author: kim-simons
prepTime: 20
coolTime: 270
servings: 8
diet: Vegetarian

ingredients:
  list:
    - Avocados
    - Bananas
    - Coconut cream
    - Lemon
    - Maple syrup (real)
    - Dark chocolate chips

  qty:
    - 1 fresh
    - 1 peeled
    - 1&frasl;3 cup
    - 1 squeezed
    - 3 tbsp
    - 20 oz
---

import img1 from '@assets/recipes/avocado-banana-frozen-treats/filling.jpg';
import img2 from '@assets/recipes/avocado-banana-frozen-treats/step-two-chocolate.jpg';

- Cut up the bananas and avocados.
- Add all the ingredients together in a blender and blend until smooth!
- Pour mixture in silicone molds, freeze for four hours.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Blended green avocado-banana mixture being placed into molds" />
- Melt dark chocolate in microwave at 15-minute intervals, stirring each time until melted.
- Remove avocado banana ice cream from mold and coat with chocolate.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="Avocado-banana frozen treats being coated with melted dark chocolate in a glass bowl" />
- Place on a parchment paper-lined cookie sheet, and freeze until the chocolate sets.
