---
title: Chocolate Ganache
description: A rich, velvety chocolate ganache made with semi-sweet chocolate and heavy cream, perfect as a glaze or filling for cakes and pastries.
image: "@assets/recipes/chocolate-ganache/ganache.jpg"
imageAlt: "Thick, glossy dark chocolate being poured from a glass bowl into a piping bag fitted with a metal tip"
author: kim-simons
prepTime: 10
cookTime: 5
coolTime: 120
diet: Vegetarian

ingredients:
  list:
    - Semi-sweet chocolate pieces
    - Heavy cream or heavy whipping cream

  qty:
    - 1 cup
    - 1 cup (8 ounces; 240ml)

notes:
  - For a thicker ganache use a 2-to-1 ratio (2 cups) of semi-sweet chocolate pieces
---

import img2 from "@assets/recipes/chocolate-ganache/cream-with-chocolate.jpg";
import img3 from "@assets/recipes/chocolate-ganache/mixing-ganache.jpg";
import img4 from "@assets/recipes/chocolate-ganache/mixed-ganache.jpg";

- Heat the cream in a medium, microwave safe bowl for about 2 minutes until cream is very warm
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Glass bowl of cream heating in a microwave" />
- Add chocolate.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="Glass bowl with cream and chocolate pieces added" />
- Let sit for 2-3 minutes to gently soften the chocolate.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Whisking the cream and chocolate mixture together" />
- With a metal whisk, stir until completely combined and chocolate is totally melted and smooth.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Smooth, glossy chocolate ganache being whisked in a glass bowl" />
- Ganache is ready to use as a drizzle, or let it cool and thicken by letting it sit at room temperature (it will fully cool within 2 hours; refrigerating will speed this up).
