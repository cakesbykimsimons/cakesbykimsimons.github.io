- Preheat oven to 375°F. Melt butter and set aside.
- Combine flour, cocoa, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl. Mix butter, brown sugar, granulated sugar, and vanilla extract in a large mixing bowl.
- Add eggs one at a time to the butter mixture, mixing well after each addition. Gradually pour into the flour mixture.
- Roll dough into 2-inch balls and place onto a parchment-lined baking sheet. Flatten dough slightly, then press Halloween candy pieces into the top.
- Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, or until cookies are puffed.
- Cool on the baking sheet for 2 minutes, then remove to a wire rack to cool completely.
