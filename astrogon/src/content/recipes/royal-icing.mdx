---
title: Royal Icing
description: A classic, smooth icing that dries hard, perfect for decorating cookies and gingerbread houses, made with meringue powder, confectioners' sugar, and water.
image: "@assets/how-tos/mountain-view-cookie/mountain-view-cookie.jpg"
imageAlt: "A gingerbread cookie decorated with a landscape painting featuring white birch trees, a blue sky, and pink and yellow flower details"
author: kim-simons
prepTime: 15

ingredients:
  list:
  - Meringue Powder
  - Confectioners' sugar
  - Warm water

  qty:
  - 3 tbsp
  - 4 cups (about 1 lb)
  - 6 tbsp

notes:
  - Keep all utensils completely grease-free for proper icing consistency.
  - For stiffer icing (or when using a large countertop mixer), use 1 tablespoon less water.
---

- Beat all ingredients until icing forms peaks (7-10 minutes at low speed with a heavy-duty mixer, 10-12 minutes at high speed with a hand-held mixer).
- To thin for pouring, add 1 teaspoon water per cup of royal icing. Use a grease-free spoon or spatula to stir slowly, adding 1/2 teaspoon water at a time until you reach the proper consistency.
