---
title: Chocolate Ganache
description: A rich, velvety chocolate ganache made with semi-sweet chocolate and heavy cream, perfect as a glaze or filling for cakes and pastries.
image: "@assets/blog/make-this-candle-cake-centerpiece/candle-cake.jpg"
author: kim-simons
prepTime: 10
cook_time: 5
diet: Vegetarian

ingredients:
  list:
    - Semi-sweet chocolate pieces
    - Heavy cream or heavy whipping cream

  qty:
    - 1 cup
    - 1 cup (8 ounces; 240ml)

instructions:
  - Heat the cream in a medium, microwave safe bowl for about 2 minutes until cream is very warm, then add chocolate.
  - Let sit for 2-3 minutes to gently soften the chocolate.
  - With a metal whisk, stir until completely combined and chocolate is totally melted and smooth. Ganache is ready to use as a drizzle, or let it cool and thicken by letting it sit at room temperature (it will fully cool within 2 hours; refrigerating will speed this up).


notes:
  - For a thicker ganache use a 2-to-1 ratio (2 cups) of semi-sweet chocolate pieces
---
