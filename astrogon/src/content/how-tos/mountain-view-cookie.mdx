- Make a sugar cookie or gingerbread using your favorite recipe.
- Make and color Royal Icing as instructed.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Bowl with white, blue, green, and orange royal icing for decorating." />
- Spread and blend Royal Icing on the top half of the cookie with a spatula to make the sky.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="Knife spreading blue and white royal icing sky onto a square cookie." />
- Use a small palette knife to add green icing for the grass.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Knife spreading green royal icing for grass on the cookie." />
- Add trees to the center using a palette knife and a small brush.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Hand using a brush to add green royal icing trees to the cookie." />
- Spread and blend icing on the bottom half of the cookie to make the field.
- Add mountains to the top half using a palette knife and a brush.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="Hand detailing the mountain and field scene on the cookie with a brush." />
- Add color to the foreground with a small brush, using a horizontal motion.
- Add birch trees using a small brush and white icing.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="Hand using a paintbrush to add white birch tree details with icing." />
- Detail the trees and background with black icing.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Hand adding black royal icing branch details to the birch trees." />
- Make flowers and leaves out of edible paper.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="Hand punching edible paper flowers in pink, purple, and yellow." />
- Assemble using piping gel.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="Hand assembling edible paper flowers with piping gel." />
- Glue the plants to the foreground with icing.
  <StepImage src={img10} alt="Hand gluing edible paper flowers to the cookie foreground." />
