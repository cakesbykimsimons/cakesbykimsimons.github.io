- Soften modeling chocolate by kneading until smooth.
- Make a ball (about 1-inch diameter) for the base of the rose.
- Make four 1/4-inch balls for the first set of petals, three 1/2-inch balls for the second set, and five 3/4-inch balls for the third set.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Graduated pink modeling chocolate balls for rose petals on a surface." />
- Pinch the 1/4-inch balls until flat, then pinch the edges to add texture.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="Hands shaping a pink modeling chocolate ball into a flat petal." />
- Take the first petal, attach to the 1-inch ball, and form a cone (may require a little water).
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Hand attaching the first modeling chocolate petal to form the rose center." />
- Flip the petal over and press your thumb into the center to shape it.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Hand pressing a thumb into a pink modeling chocolate petal to shape it." />
- Take a flattened petal, curve it, and roll the opposite edges with a toothpick.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="Hand using a toothpick to curve the edges of a modeling chocolate petal." />
- Add petals one at a time, keeping the height slightly higher than the center cone. Overlap each petal. Use a smoothing tool to attach. Curl back the tips with your fingers.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="Hand adding layered modeling chocolate petals to the rose." />
- Repeat with the three 1/2-inch balls for the second set and five 3/4-inch balls for the third set. Continue until the rose is the desired size.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Hands holding a completed layered pink modeling chocolate rose." />
- Dust the rose with petal dust to add dimension.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="Hand dusting the pink modeling chocolate rose with petal dust using a brush." />
- Pipe buttercream onto the cupcake.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="Hand piping pink buttercream swirl onto a chocolate cupcake." />
- Add sprinkles.
  <StepImage src={img10} alt="Cupcake with pink buttercream topped with colorful sprinkles." />
- Add the rose to the cupcake.
  <StepImage src={img11} alt="Hands holding a cupcake topped with a pink modeling chocolate rose and sprinkles." />
