---
title: Gingerbread Tree of Life
description: Learn how to create a gingerbread Tree of Life decoration using a simple gingerbread dough, wafer paper flowers, and edible petal dust.
image: "@assets/blog/make-this-gingerbread-tree-of-life/20230621-131607-25fd-_kim-_main.jpg"
author: kim-simons
skillLevel: Beginner
supplies:
  list:
    - Favorite gingerbread recipe and ingredients
    - Colored wafer paper (I prefer Icing Images)
    - Paper potion (I prefer Icing Images)
    - "Petal dust: Dark brown and white (I prefer The Sugar Art)"
    - Piping gel
    - Cookie sheet
    - Parchment paper
    - Silicone mat
    - Cake tools
    - Medium paint brush
    - 1" circle hole punch
    - Oven
notes:
  - Bake in 5-minute intervals to prevent burning. The piece is done when golden brown and dry to the touch.
---

- Preheat oven to 350°F (or to temperature recommended in your gingerbread recipe). Mix up dough according to the recipe, and roll dough into a long, thin cylinder.
- Cut a long piece of dough. On parchment paper, shape it into a frame.
- Add pieces of dough, cut into various lengths, into the frame to look like tree branches.
- Add details with cake tools.
- Add further details.
- Bake in 5-minute intervals until golden brown and dry to the touch.
- Using a paper punch, create little circles of various colors out of wafer paper.
- Shape the circles into little flowers.
- Add the flowers to the tree with piping gel.
- Dust the piece with edible petal dust.
