- Line a cookie sheet with plastic wrap and lay out edible paper.
- Using a small amount of water, mix up the blue edible colors in two small bowls.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Mixing blue edible colors in small bowls." />
- With a paintbrush or dropper, color the paper randomly. Set aside to dry, preferably overnight.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="Dripping blue edible color onto edible paper." />
- Rip the paper into horizontal strips.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Ripping colored edible paper into strips." />
- Using piping gel, attach the designed paper to your pre-prepared, fondant-covered cake, working from the top down on the lower layer.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Applying colored paper strips to the lower cake tier." />
- Repeat on the top layer.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="Applying colored paper strips to the top cake tier." />
- Paint your cake board blue.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="Painting the cake board blue with a brush." />
- Decorate with luster dust.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Spraying gold luster dust onto the cake." />
- Add pre-prepared wafer flower decorations.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="Placing wafer flower decorations on the cake." />
