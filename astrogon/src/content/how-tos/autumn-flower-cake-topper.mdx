- Take some aluminum foil and build a base to shape the petals.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="" />
- Take two differently colored sheets of wafer paper.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="" />
- Spray one with Paper Potion. Stick the sheets together.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="" />
- Trim edges with scissors.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="" />
- Paint one side with clear piping gel.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="" />
- Roll in the edges and fold your petal into shape. Place it on foil to dry.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="" />
- Repeat process to make more petals.
- Scrunch the paper in the center, then cut four petals with scissors. Shape petals and set on foil to dry.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="" />
- Lay out petal pieces, then glue together with clear isomalt, working from back to front.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="" />
- Repeat process until flower is built.
- Take gold Jewel Color and add sparkle.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="" />
- Add flower to your cake.
