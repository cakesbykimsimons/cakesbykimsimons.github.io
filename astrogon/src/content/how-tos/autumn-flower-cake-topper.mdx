title: Autumn Flower Cake Topper
description: Learn how to create a stunning autumn flower cake topper using wafer paper, clear isomalt, and gold jewel color. A beautiful edible decoration for fall cakes and cupcakes.

author: kim-simons
skillLevel: Beginner
supplies:
  list:
    - Wafer paper in your choice of colors (I prefer Icing Images)
    - Gold Jewel Color (from The Sweet Chalet)
    - Paper Potion (I prefer Icing Images)
    - Piping gel
    - Aluminum foil
    - Scissors
    - Paintbrush
    - Small silicone cup
    - Clear isomalt (I prefer SimiCakes)
    - Microwave
    - Silicone mat
---

- Take some aluminum foil and build a base to shape the petals.
- Take two differently colored sheets of wafer paper and spray one with Paper Potion. Stick the sheets together.
- Trim edges with scissors.
- Paint one side with clear piping gel.
- Roll in the edges and fold your petal into shape. Place it on foil to dry.
- Repeat process to make more petals.
- Scrunch the paper in the center, then cut four petals with scissors. Shape petals and set on foil to dry.
- Lay out petal pieces, then glue together with clear isomalt, working from back to front.
- Repeat process until flower is built.
- Take gold Jewel Color and add sparkle.
- Add flower to your cake.
