- Heat melting chocolate chips in microwave for 30 seconds, then in 15-second intervals until ready to stir.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="" />
- Stir chocolate and pour into molds. Be careful to coat the sides. Let harden.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="" />
- Make the peanut butter filling: Place butter into a bowl. Stir in the peanut butter and the salt. Mix well.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="" />
- Add the confectioners sugar and mix. Add water and mix until totally combined.
- Pour filling out onto parchment paper. Fold paper over the top of filling. Roll flat with rolling pin.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="" />
- Using cookie cutters, cut pieces of filling and add to chocolate in molds.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="" />
- Repeat with pieces of cake (your choice of flavor).
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="" />
- Melt more chocolate, and pour over the top of the cake. Let harden.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="" />
- Pop morsel out of mold.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="" />
- Decorate and enjoy!
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="" />
