- Using a microwave, temper white chocolate wafers.
  - Heat for 30 seconds.
    <StepImage src={img1} alt="White chocolate melting wafers in a glass bowl inside a microwave." />
  - Then continue heating for 15-second intervals.
  - Stir the chocolate in between bursts to ensure it melts evenly.
    <StepImage src={img2} alt="A hand stirring melted white chocolate in a glass bowl." />
  - Temper until chocolate is 87-88 degrees Fahrenheit.
- Pour tempered chocolate into pumpkin mold.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Pouring tempered white chocolate into a silicone pumpkin mold." />
- Swirl the mold slowly to coat the inside. Pour off excess and let harden.
- Carefully remove hardened chocolate from the mold.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Pressing the silicone mold to release the hardened chocolate pumpkin." />
- Temper more white chocolate and shape into a stem.
- Pour your favorite candy into the chocolate pumpkin.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="Pouring colorful candies into the hollow chocolate pumpkin shell." />
- Place the stem on top. Seal with tempered chocolate.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="Placing the chocolate stem on the pumpkin and sealing with tempered chocolate." />
- Detail surface using a clay tool and a small precision knife.
- Paint and dust with edible colors.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Painting the chocolate pumpkin with orange edible color using a paintbrush." />
