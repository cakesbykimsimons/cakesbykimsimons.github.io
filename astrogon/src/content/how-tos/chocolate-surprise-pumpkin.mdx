supplies:
  list:
    - Silicone pumpkin mold
    - White chocolate melting wafers (I prefer Ghirardelli)
    - "Chocolate Chameleon colors (I prefer the Sweet Chalet): peach or orange and lime green"
    - "Edible dusting powder (I prefer the Sugar Art): shades of oranges and greens"
    - Candy thermometer
    - Plastic or glass bowl
    - Stirring spoon
    - Microwave
    - Paint brushes
    - Clay tool
    - Silicone mat
    - Water
notes:
  - Temper white chocolate to 87-88 degrees Fahrenheit for best results.
  - Heat for 30 seconds, then in 15-second intervals, stirring between bursts.

- Using a microwave, temper white chocolate wafers. Heat for 30 seconds, then for 15-second intervals. Temper until chocolate is 87-88 degrees Fahrenheit.
- Pour tempered chocolate into pumpkin mold. Swirl the mold slowly to coat the inside. Pour off excess and let harden.
- Carefully remove hardened chocolate from the mold.
- Temper more white chocolate and shape into a stem.
- Pour your favorite candy into the chocolate pumpkin. Place the stem on top. Seal with tempered chocolate.
- Detail surface using clay tool and X-Acto knife.
- Paint and dust with edible colors (I prefer products from Sugar Art and the Sweet Chalet).
