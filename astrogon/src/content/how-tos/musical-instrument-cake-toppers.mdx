- Start with your favorite gingerbread cookie recipe. Mix up the ingredients and roll out the dough.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="" />
- Place musical instrument stencil on rolled out dough. Cut to shape with a sharp blade. Repeat as necessary.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="" />
- Bake shaped cookies as per your recipe.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="" />
- Roll out fondant. Cover instrument cookies with fondant sheets. Trim excess.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="" />
- Sculpt details out of fondant and add to cookies as needed.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="" />
- Draw on any necessary details with a pen using edible ink.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="" />
- Paint cookies to your preference with edible paint.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="" />
