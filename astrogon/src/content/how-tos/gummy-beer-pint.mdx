---
title: Gummy Beer Pint
description: Learn how to make a gummy beer that looks like a real pint but tastes like orange candy. A fun gelatin confection that fools everyone.
image: "@assets/how-tos/gummy-beer-pint/gummy-beer-pint.jpg"
imageAlt: "A gummy candy replica of a beer pint with golden liquid and white foam"
author: kim-simons
skillLevel: Beginner
supplies:
  list:
    - 7 Gelatin Packets
    - 2 Cups Water
    - Silicone Cup Or Medium Glass Bowl
    - Large Paper Cup
    - 6 Drops Candy Flavoring Oil
    - Spoons
    - Microwave
    - Glass Bowl
    - Small precision knife
    - Immersion Blender
notes:
  - Do NOT overheat the gelatin mixture
  - Be careful when cutting the cup
  - Feel free to experiment with any flavors you like
---

- Sprinkle gelatin over the water.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Hands sprinkle gelatin powder over water in a glass measuring cup." />
- Use a spoon to push the gelatin down into the water.
- Add 6 drops of flavoring into the gelatin mixture and stir.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="A hand pours candy flavoring oil into the gelatin mixture in a glass cup." />
- Add color to the gelatin mixture.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Hands hold orange and yellow colorant bottles beside the gelatin mixture." />
- Stir and let set about 5 minutes.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="A hand stirs the gelatin mixture as it begins to set in a glass measuring cup." />
- Microwave for 30-second increments, stirring in between until mixture is clear and there are no visible granules. Do NOT overheat.
- Pour mixture into large paper cup about 3/4 of the way.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="A hand pours the melted gelatin mixture into a large paper cup." />
- Pour leftover gelatin in a small bowl.
- Melt leftover gelatin. Dissolve in microwave in 30 second increments.
- Use immersion blender or whisk to make bubbles in gelatin.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="An immersion blender creates foam in the leftover gelatin mixture." />
- Spoon foam into cup with the chilled gelatin mixture and let set about 15 minutes.
- Remove the gummy beer from the cup — use a small precision knife to cut the cup and peel it off the gelatin beer.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Hands use a precision knife to cut the paper cup and release the gummy beer." />
- Reheat the leftover gelatin and add the foam to the top. Let some drip down the sides.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="A spoon adds white gelatin foam dripping down the side of the gummy pint." />
