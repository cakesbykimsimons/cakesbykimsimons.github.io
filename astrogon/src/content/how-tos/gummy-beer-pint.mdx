---
title: Gummy Beer Pint
description: Learn how to make a gummy beer that looks like a real pint but tastes like orange candy. A fun gelatin confection that fools everyone.
image: "@assets/how-tos/gummy-beer-pint/gummy-beer-pint.jpg"
author: kim-simons
skillLevel: Beginner
supplies:
  list:
    - 7 Gelatin Packets
    - 2 Cups Water
    - Silicone Cup Or Medium Glass Bowl
    - Large Paper Cup
    - 6 Drops Candy Flavoring Oil
    - Spoons
    - Microwave
    - Glass Bowl
    - Utility knife
    - Immersion Blender
notes:
  - Do NOT overheat the gelatin mixture
  - Be careful when cutting the cup
  - Feel free to experiment with any flavors you like

- Sprinkle gelatin over the water. Use a spoon to push the gelatin down into the water.
- Add 6 drops of flavoring into the gelatin mixture and stir.
- Add color to the gelatin mixture.
- Stir and let set about 5 minutes.
- Microwave for 30-second increments, stirring in between until mixture is clear and there are no visible granules. Do NOT overheat. Pour mixture into large paper cup about 3/4 of the way. Pour leftover gelatin in a small bowl.
- Melt leftover gelatin. Dissolve in microwave in 30 second increments. Use immersion blender or whisk to make bubbles in gelatin.
- Spoon foam into cup with the chilled gelatin mixture and let set about 15 minutes. Remove the gummy beer from the cup — use a utility knife to cut the cup and peel it off the gelatin beer.
- Reheat the leftover gelatin and add the foam to the top. Let some drip down the sides.
