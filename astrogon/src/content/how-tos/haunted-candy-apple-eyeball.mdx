- Clean and thoroughly dry apples.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Hands wipe an apple clean with a paper towel." />
- Insert a wooden stick.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="A hand inserts a wooden skewer into the top of the apple." />
- Heat up your favorite hard candy: 30 seconds in microwave, then 15-second intervals until it boils. Be careful — candy will be very hot.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Melted red hard candy in a glass measuring cup." />
- Dip apples into melted candy. Remove the excess and let harden.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="A hand dips a skewered apple into melted red candy coating." />
- Heat up clear isomalt as you did the candy. Pour into mold. Use sugar torch to eliminate bubbles. Let harden.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="Clear isomalt being poured into a half-sphere silicone mold." />
- Paint the eyeball on the isomalt with edible colors. Pour on another layer of clear isomalt. Let harden. Paint in details on the eye — veins, etc. Pour on another layer of isomalt. Let harden.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="A hand paints the eyeball iris and details onto the isomalt with a brush." />
- Heat more candy. Pour onto silicone mat. Manipulate the mat to create a stem for the eye. Attach stem to apple.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Hands manipulate melted candy on a silicone mat to form a stem shape." />
- Heat up and pour layer of white isomalt onto back of eye. Let harden. Remove from mold. Use sugar torch to remove any bubbles.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="White isomalt being poured onto the back of the eyeball in the mold." />
- Heat apple with sugar torch and attach the eye.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="Hands attach the finished isomalt eyeball to the candy apple." />
