---
title: Van Gogh Bunnies
description: Learn how to paint van Gogh-inspired bunnies on edible SmArt Sheets. A fun, creative project for edible art on cakes or displays.
image: "@assets/how-tos/van-gogh-bunnies/van-gogh-bunnies.jpg"
author: kim-simons
skillLevel: Beginner
supplies:
  list:
    - SmArt Sheets edible paper (from Icing Images)
    - Edible Lace Fabric Sheets (from Icing Images)
    - Edible gel colors (I prefer Artisan Accents)
    - 10 small mixing bowls
    - 10 spoons
    - Palette knife
    - Paper towels
    - Water
notes:
  - The fabric sheet mixture should be the consistency of chocolate syrup when dissolved.
  - Use a palette knife or brush, whichever you prefer.
---

- Cut or tear your fabric sheets.
- Place the strips in a small bowl.
- Add water (about a tablespoon) to dilute, then stir until dissolved (it should be the consistency of chocolate syrup when ready).
- Add the desired color using Artisan Accents edible gel colors.
- Using a palette knife, start painting on the SmArt Sheet. You may use a brush if you prefer.
- Have fun swirling your color choices! Add black for the starry night look.
- Sketch in your bunnies.
- Add shadows and details to your bunnies. You can use your finished piece as a display or to wrap a cake!
