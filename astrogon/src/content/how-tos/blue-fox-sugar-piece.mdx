supplies:
  list:
    - Blue, white, and black fondant (I prefer Satin Ice)
    - "Edible colors: navy blue (I prefer The Sweet Chalet Artisan Accent Colors)"
    - "Edible powder colors: navy blue (I prefer The Sugar Art)"
    - Cake tools
    - Rolling pin
    - Ball tool
    - Silicone mat
    - X-Acto blade
    - Scissors
    - Paint brush
    - Water
---

- Roll a ball of blue fondant a little larger than a golf ball. Shape as desired, and use cake tool to carve out and shape arms and legs.
- Roll out and shape a second piece of fondant for the head. Attach it to the body. Add eye sockets using a ball tool.
- Roll out a smaller cylinder of fondant. Snip in half with scissors. Shape each into an ear and add to the head. Smooth with cake tool.
- Roll out a larger cylinder of fondant, shape into a bushy tail, and add to body.
- Roll out lighter-colored fondant thinly. Snip into shapes that will fit into the eye sockets, ears, and chest area. Add each piece and smooth out with ball and cake tools. Trim as needed with an X-Acto knife.
- Roll out another thin piece of lighter-colored fondant. Trim to resemble the tuft of a tail with scissors. Add to tail, and trim any excess with X-Acto blade. Roll out three tiny balls of black fondant. Add to face as eyes and nose.
- Roll out an extremely thin cylinder of darker fondant. Outline ears and eyes, trimming away any excess.
- Shadow using edible petal dust.
