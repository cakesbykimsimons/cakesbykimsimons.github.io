- Sprinkle gelatin over the water. Use a spoon to push the gelatin down into the water.
- Add 6 drops of flavoring into the gelatin mixture and stir.
- Add color to the gelatin mixture.
- Stir and let set about 5 minutes.
- Microwave for 30-second increments, stirring in between until mixture is clear and there are no visible granules. Do NOT overheat. Pour mixture into large paper cup about 3/4 of the way. Pour leftover gelatin in a small bowl.
- Melt leftover gelatin. Dissolve in microwave in 30 second increments. Use immersion blender or whisk to make bubbles in gelatin.
- Spoon foam into cup with the chilled gelatin mixture and let set about 15 minutes. Remove the gummy beer from the cup — use an X-Acto blade to cut the cup and peel it off the gelatin beer.
- Reheat the leftover gelatin and add the foam to the top. Let some drip down the sides.
