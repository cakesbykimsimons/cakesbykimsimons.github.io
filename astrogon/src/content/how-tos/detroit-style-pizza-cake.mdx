---
title: Detroit-Style Pizza Cake
description: Turn a sheet cake into a realistic Detroit-style pizza cake with ganache, fondant, and a blowtorch for that crispy-edged look.
image: "@assets/blog/make-this-pelkey-s-tavern-detroit-style-pizza-cake/20231227-110859-52fd-_Kim-_main__1_.jpg"
author: kim-simons
skillLevel: Intermediate
supplies:
  list:
    - 12" x 12" x 1" Cake, Any Flavor
    - Chocolate Ganache
    - Buttercream
    - Fondant – White (I Prefer Smartflex Velvet)
    - Red and Orange Color (I Prefer Artisan Accent Colors)
    - Petal Dust (I Prefer the Sugar Art Colors)
    - Sugar Torch
    - Silicone Mat
    - Rolling Pin
    - Wheel Cutter
    - Cake Tools
    - Offset Spatulas
    - Paint Brushes
    - Parchment Paper
    - Pizza Box
    - Water
notes:
  - Use a blowtorch to brown fondant for a baked pizza crust effect.
  - Work in stages so fondant layers can set before torching.
---
- Bake your favorite cake: 12" x 12" x 1".
- Cover cake with chocolate ganache.
- Roll out fondant and make cake toppings (pepperoni, etc.).
- Add buttercream icing to cake.
- Roll out thin sheet of fondant; cover cake. Trim excess.
- Cover cake with "chips" of fondant.
- Brown with blowtorch.
- Add fondant toppings. Shape with ball tool; brown with torch.
- Add "sauce" (buttercream icing with edible colors). Brown with torch.
- Dust edges with edible paints; brown with torch.
