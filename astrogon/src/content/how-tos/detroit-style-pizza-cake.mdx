- Bake your favorite cake: 12" x 12" x 1".
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="A square baked cake on parchment paper." />
- Cover cake with chocolate ganache.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="Spreading chocolate ganache over the square sheet cake." />
- Roll out fondant and make cake toppings (pepperoni, etc.).
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Rolling out red fondant to make pepperoni toppings." />
- Add buttercream icing to cake.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Spreading buttercream icing onto the cake." />
- Roll out thin sheet of fondant; cover cake. Trim excess.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="Draping a sheet of white fondant over the crumb-coated cake." />
- Cover cake with "chips" of fondant.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="Pressing fondant chips onto the cake for texture." />
- Brown with blowtorch.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Using a blowtorch to brown the fondant for a baked crust effect." />
- Add fondant toppings. Shape with ball tool; brown with torch.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="Browning the fondant pizza toppings with a blowtorch." />
- Add "sauce" (buttercream icing with edible colors). Brown with torch.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="Applying red buttercream sauce to the pizza cake." />
- Dust edges with edible paints; brown with torch.
  <StepImage src={img10} alt="Dusting the pizza cake edges with edible paints." />
