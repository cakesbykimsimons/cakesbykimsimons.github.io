- Make about 18 1-inch balls out of modeling chocolate.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Bowl of modeling chocolate balls" />
- Roll the balls thin, about 11 inches long and about 1/4 inch thick.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="Rolling modeling chocolate into long strips" />
- Lay three strips over the bottom of the bowl.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Modeling chocolate strips laid over an inverted bowl" />
- Use a simple weave pattern to form the basket.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Woven modeling chocolate strips forming a basket pattern" />
- Use a little water to cement the connections, twist off the tips, and sculpt the rim.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="Sculpting the rim of the woven chocolate basket" />
- Use a clay tool to add texture and refrigerate for at least 15 minutes.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="Textured woven chocolate basket after chilling" />
- Use the petal punch to make flowers.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Small white fondant flowers made with a petal punch" />
- Pull the basket out of the Styrofoam bowl.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="Removing the chocolate basket from the Styrofoam mold" />
- Paint the basket with brown petal dust.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="Painting the chocolate basket with brown petal dust" />
- Add flowers with a little water, then paint with petal dust.
  <StepImage src={img10} alt="Adding white flowers to the painted chocolate basket" />
- Cut grass strips out of wafer paper and lay in the basket.
- Add candy.
