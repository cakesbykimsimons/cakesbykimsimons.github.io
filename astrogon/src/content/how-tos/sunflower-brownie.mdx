---
title: Sunflower Brownie
description: Learn how to make a stunning gumpaste sunflower decoration to top your favorite frosted brownies. A fun and creative baking project for spring and summer.
image: "@assets/blog/make-this-sunflower-brownie/20220825-124800-34fd-_Kim-_main.jpg"
author: kim-simons
skillLevel: Beginner
supplies:
  list:
    - Your favorite brownies, frosted
    - Gumpaste
    - Edible color (yellow)
    - Edible petal dust (orange)
    - Sunflower cookie cutter
    - 4" cookie cutter
    - Fondant forming cups
    - Piping gel
    - Chocolate sprinkles
    - Cake tools
    - Wide offset spatula
    - Silicone mat
    - Small rolling pin
    - Cornstarch
    - Paint brushes
notes:
  - The sunflower needs to dry overnight before adding to the brownie.
---

- Roll out gumpaste thinly. Tip: use corn starch.
- Cut sunflower out of gumpaste using a sunflower cookie cutter. Tip: dust the cookie cutter with corn starch.
- Lift sunflower off mat with offset spatula and place in fondant forming cup.
- Let dry overnight.
- Remove the sunflower from the cup with a cake tool.
- Paint the sunflower with edible paint.
- Dust the sunflower with edible dust.
- Add piping gel and sprinkles to the center.
- Add sunflower to top of premade frosted brownie with piping gel.
