  list:

notes:
  - The longer you wait, the stronger the extract becomes
  - You can reuse the beans by adding fresh vodka to the same beans for a second batch

  - Use pure vanilla beans, not imitation vanilla
  - Choose a vodka with a neutral flavor profile
  - Keep the jar tightly sealed to prevent evaporation
  - Label your jar with the date you started

- Cut the vanilla beans in half lengthwise using a sharp knife. This exposes the seeds inside and allows the alcohol to extract more flavor. You don't need to scrape out the seeds — just split them open.
- Place the split vanilla beans into a clean glass jar. Pour the vodka over the beans, making sure they're completely submerged. The beans will float initially but will sink as they absorb the alcohol.
- Close the jar tightly and store it in a dark, cool place like a pantry or cabinet. Shake the jar gently every few days to help distribute the flavors.
- Let the extract steep for at least 2 months before using. After 2 months, you can strain out the beans or leave them in the jar for continued flavor extraction. The longer it steeps (up to a year), the richer the flavor becomes.
