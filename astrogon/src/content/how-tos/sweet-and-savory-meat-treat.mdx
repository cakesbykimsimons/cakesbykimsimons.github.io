- Slice your favorite savory meat thinly. Fry on each side in 5-minute intervals until crisp on both sides.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="" />
- Crush the nut topping of your choice in a plastic bag with a hammer.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="" />
- Temper the chocolate: microwave for 30 seconds, then at 15-second intervals until it reaches 113 degrees Fahrenheit. Fold in more chocolate until the temperature comes down to 88 degrees. Mix well.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="" />
- Blot the meat.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="" />
- Spoon out chocolate on top, then smooth it with a cake knife. Add a bit of cinnamon to the other side. Cover with chocolate and smooth with cake knife. Sprinkle crushed nut topping onto warm chocolate. Put in fridge and let cool until hardened.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="" />
