- Lay out transfer sheets. Grease the insides of metal cookie cutters with cooking oil.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Hand holding a rainbow gradient transfer sheet above other colorful transfer sheets." />
- Place cookie cutters on transfer sheets.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="Metal cookie cutters resting on colorful marbled transfer sheets." />
- Heat up isomalt (in your choice of colors) in silicone cup until it bubbles. Use gloves to handle isomalt.
- Pour isomalt into metal cookie cutters. Use blow torch lightly to clear bubbles. Let cool 15 to 20 minutes.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Red isomalt being poured from a container into a metal cookie cutter." />
- Remove isomalt shapes from cookie cutters. Use blow torch and/or cake tools to help.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Hand using a brush to help remove an isomalt shape from a heart-shaped cookie cutter." />
- Assemble heart pieces, gluing together with isomalt.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="Assembled marbled isomalt heart pieces being joined together." />
- Paint edges and center heart with jewel colors. Decorate with sprinkles.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="Gold glitter being sprinkled onto a painted isomalt heart piece." />
- Heat up more isomalt. Pour onto silicone mat.
- Use mat to combine, mold, and cool isomalt until it's pliable like clay. Gloves are recommended.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Hand holding warm, pliable isomalt being worked on a silicone mat." />
- Shape pieces of isomalt into wings and tail. Shape other pieces into swan's head, neck, and body. Let cool.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="Hands shaping isomalt into the swan body shape." />
- Detail wings with a detailing pen.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="Detailing pen being used to carve feather details into isomalt wings." />
- Glue wings and tail to head-neck-body with isomalt.
  <StepImage src={img10} alt="Attaching isomalt wings and tail to the swan body with warm isomalt." />
- Heat up black isomalt. Dab onto head to create eyes.
  <StepImage src={img11} alt="Applying black isomalt to create the swan's eye." />
- Glue swan to the heart piece with isomalt.
  <StepImage src={img12} alt="Finished isomalt swan glued onto the decorated heart-shaped base." />
