- Using your favorite sugar cookie recipe, roll the dough out and cut out bottle- and glass-shaped cookies using cookie cutters or stencils. Bake until golden brown.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Baked wine bottle and wine glass shaped cookies on a mat." />
- Roll out modeling chocolate in the colors of your choice.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="Hands rolling out green modeling chocolate with a rolling pin." />
- Attach modeling chocolate to your bottle cookies with piping gel. Trim the edges to fit.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Hand trimming green modeling chocolate around a bottle-shaped cookie." />
- Repeat the process on your glass cookies.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Hand holding a cookie cutter over rolled modeling chocolate for the glass cookie." />
- Print labels for the bottle and any design for the glass on edible paper using edible ink. Cut out printed labels and designs on edible wafer paper with scissors.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="Hand holding a printed edible wafer paper wine label over a green cookie." />
- Attach labels and designs to cookies with piping gel.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="Hands applying an edible label to the modeling chocolate bottle cookie." />
- Add highlights to cookies with petal dust.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Hand using a fine brush to add white petal dust highlights to the bottle cookie." />
- Add details to cookies with petal dust.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="Hand adding petal dust details to the wine glass and bottle cookies." />
- Add shadows to cookies with petal dust. Your bottle and glass cookies are ready to be enjoyed.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="Hand adding shadow details with petal dust to the bottle and glass cookies." objectPosition="center bottom" />
