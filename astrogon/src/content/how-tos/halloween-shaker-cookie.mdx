- Roll out sugar cookie dough about a quarter-inch thick.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="A hand rolls sugar cookie dough with a rolling pin on a silicone mat." />
- Using a poster board template and a small precision knife, cut out designs. Make two opposite pieces.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="A paper template and knife are used to cut Halloween cookie shapes from dough." />
- Make the inner piece by cutting a border, about 3/4-inch all the way around.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="A hand cuts the inner border of a Halloween cookie shape with a precision knife." />
- Using parchment paper, put on baking sheet and bake cookie as directed in your recipe. Let cool.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Baked Halloween cookie shapes cooling on a silicone baking mat." />
- Sketch out your design on the top piece.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="A hand holds a Halloween cookie with a sketched cat-on-pumpkin design." />
- Using Royal Icing, decorate your top cookie.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="A hand pipes royal icing onto the Halloween top cookie." />
- On the opposite piece, use yellow Royal Icing to fill in to the edges.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Yellow royal icing fills the back cookie piece to the edges." />
- Add border piece on top of back piece (to be sandwiched in the middle); set aside to let dry.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="A border of royal icing is added to the back cookie to create a chamber." />
- Add highlights and shadows by dusting with edible powder color.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="A brush dusts edible powder highlights onto the decorated top cookie." />
- When cookie is totally dry and hardened, heat up your isomalt. Pour a thin layer of isomalt into the mouth, eyes, and nose areas of both cookies (wear gloves — isomalt is very hot!). Let cool.
  <StepImage src={img10} alt="Melted isomalt is poured into the cutout windows of the cookie." />
- Take bottom cookie piece and add Halloween sprinkles.
  <StepImage src={img11} alt="Halloween sprinkles are added to the bottom cookie chamber." />
- Using some more Royal Icing on the outer ridge of the bottom cookie, glue the top cookie. Let dry, and shake!
  <StepImage src={img12} alt="The top cookie is glued onto the bottom cookie with royal icing." />
