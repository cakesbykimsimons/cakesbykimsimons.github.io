---
title: Holiday Gnome Out of Fondant
description: Learn how to sculpt a festive holiday gnome using fondant over a Rice Krispie treat base. A fun Christmas project for bakers of all skill levels.
image: "@assets/blog/make-this-holiday-gnome-out-of-fondant/20221222-083320-51fd-_Kim-_main.jpg"
author: kim-simons
skillLevel: Beginner
supplies:
  list:
    - "Fondant: green, your choice of color for skin, white, and black (I prefer Satin Ice)"
    - Cereal treats
    - Cake tools
    - Rolling pin
    - X-Acto blade
    - Toothpicks
    - Water
    - Silicone mat
    - Paint brushes
    - Edible powdered colors for dusting (I prefer The Sugar Art Colors)
---

- Shape the body, head and hat out of Rice Krispie treats.
- Roll out green fondant, cut with utility knife, and cover the body.
- Roll some fondant (your choice of color) into a ball and add to the face as a nose.
- Roll out and cut some more green fondant. Cover the hat.
- Make arms out of fondant. Attach to body.
- Make hands out of fondant. Attach to arms.
- Roll out strips of fondant for beard. Attach with cake tool.
- Roll out balls of fondant for boots. Attach to body.
