- Shape the body, head and hat out of cereal treats.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="" />
- Roll out green fondant, cut with utility knife, and cover the body.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="" />
- Roll some fondant (your choice of color) into a ball and add to the face as a nose.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="" />
- Roll out and cut some more green fondant. Cover the hat.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="" />
- Make arms out of fondant. Attach to body.
- Make hands out of fondant. Attach to arms.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="" />
- Roll out strips of fondant for beard. Attach with cake tool.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="" />
- Roll out balls of fondant for boots. Attach to body.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="" />
