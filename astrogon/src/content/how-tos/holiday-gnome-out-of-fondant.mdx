- Shape the body, head and hat out of cereal treats.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="A hand shapes a Rice Krispie treat into the gnome body form." />
- Roll out green fondant, cut with a small precision knife, and cover the body.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="A hand trims green fondant covering the gnome body with a cake tool." />
- Roll some fondant (your choice of color) into a ball and add to the face as a nose.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="A hand attaches a round fondant nose to the gnome face." />
- Roll out and cut some more green fondant. Cover the hat.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Hands press green fondant to cover the gnome hat shape." />
- Make arms out of fondant. Attach to body.
- Make hands out of fondant. Attach to arms.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="A hand uses a cake tool to shape fondant arms on the gnome body." />
- Roll out strips of fondant for beard. Attach with cake tool.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="White fondant beard strips being added to the gnome figure." />
- Roll out balls of fondant for boots. Attach to body.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Hands attach black fondant boots to the bottom of the gnome." />
