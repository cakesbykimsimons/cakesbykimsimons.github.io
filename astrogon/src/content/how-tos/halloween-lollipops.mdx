- Open edible Cello Sheets with pre-printed Halloween designs
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Three Halloween edible cello sheets with black cat, haunted house, and jack-o'-lantern." />
- Remove the backing.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="A hand peels the backing off a Halloween printed cello sheet." />
- Cut out the images with scissors.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Hands cut out Halloween images from printed cello sheets with scissors." />
- Place images in silicone lollipop molds.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Hands place Halloween cutouts into blue silicone lollipop molds." />
- Heat up clear isomalt with added candy flavoring in the microwave: 30 seconds initially, then in 15-second intervals until it bubbles.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="A hand holds a bag of clear isomalt crystals beside silicone lollipop molds." />
- Pour isomalt evenly into the molds and let cool.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="Melted isomalt is poured from a cup into silicone lollipop molds." />
- Use a blow torch to remove bubbles from lollipops. Let cool.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="A hand uses a blow torch to remove bubbles from isomalt lollipops in molds." />
- Test with a cake tool to see if lollipops have hardened.
- Remove your lollipops from the molds.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="Hands peel finished isomalt lollipops out of blue silicone molds." />
- Dip the tip of the lollipop sticks into the warm isomalt and place on the back of your lollipops. Let cool.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="Three finished Halloween isomalt lollipops resting on a silicone mat." />
- Use the blow torch to shine your lollipops one last time.
- Let cool, and enjoy!
