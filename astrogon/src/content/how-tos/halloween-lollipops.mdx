---
title: Halloween Lollipops
description: Learn how to make vibrant Halloween lollipops using isomalt and pre-printed edible cello sheets. These easy lollipops are perfect for holiday treats and gift-giving.
image: "@assets/blog/halloween-lollipops/20221025-151650-43fd-_Kim-_main.jpg"
author: kim-simons
skillLevel: Intermediate
supplies:
  list:
    - Isomalt (I prefer SimiCakes)
    - Clear Cello Sheets printed with edible Halloween images (I prefer Icing Images)
    - Candy flavoring
    - Silicone mold
    - Silicone cup
    - Silicone mat
    - Torch
    - Scissors
    - Lollipop sticks
    - Treat lollipop bags – Clear Cellophane
notes:
  - Isomalt is HOT! Use caution and wear gloves.
---

- Open edible Cello Sheets with pre-printed Halloween designs and remove backing.
- Cut out the images with scissors.
- Place images in silicone lollipop molds.
- Heat up clear isomalt with added candy flavoring in the microwave: 30 seconds initially, then in 15-second intervals until it bubbles.
- Pour isomalt evenly into the molds and let cool.
- Use a blow torch to remove bubbles from lollipops. Let cool.
- Test with a cake tool to see if lollipops have hardened.
- Remove your lollipops from the molds.
- Dip the tip of the lollipop sticks into the warm isomalt and place on the back of your lollipops. Let cool.
- Use the blow torch to shine your lollipops one last time. Let cool, and enjoy!
