- Roll out sugar cookie dough.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Rolling out sugar cookie dough with a rolling pin." />
- Shape top dough with mold and cut excess dough from shape.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="Pressing a round cookie cutter into sugar cookie dough." />
- Roll out sugar cookie dough for bottom and trim dough to shape.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Cutting sugar cookie dough rounds with a metal cutter." />
- Bake 8-10 minutes at 375 degrees Fahrenheit.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Sugar cookies baking on a sheet pan in the oven." />
- Remove mold from the top cookies.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="Removing the silicone mold from a baked cookie." />
- Using a piping bag, coat inside of top with buttercream icing and add cake.
- Cover cake with chocolate ganache.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="Piping chocolate ganache onto the cookie cake filling." />
- Put top and bottom cookies together.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Pressing the top and bottom cookies together." />
- Coat body with piping gel using a brush.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="Brushing piping gel onto the cookie body." />
- Roll out fondant thinly and cover the cookie; you now have the body.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="Stretching white fondant to cover the cookie body." />
- Roll out fondant thickly about 1/4 inch for head. Using a round cookie cutter, cut out the head.
- Using a ball tool, add the face details.
  <StepImage src={img10} alt="Using a ball tool to add face details to the fondant head." />
- Add more detail to face/body with darker fondant and glue with piping gel.
  <StepImage src={img11} alt="Decorating a fondant cow face with black spots, eyes, and a pink snout." />
- Shape horns, feet, eyes, nose, ears, etc. from fondant and glue with piping gel.
  <StepImage src={img12} alt="A pink fondant sheep head with brown horns, beard, and facial features." />
- Cut or bite your cookie and enjoy!
  <StepImage src={img13} alt="Lifting the top half of the pig cookie to reveal the layered interior." />
