- Sketch the tiger design onto the cookie with an edible marker.
  <StepImage src={img1} alt="Hand drawing a tiger face sketch on a sugar cookie with a marker." />
- Using green modeling chocolate and a clay tool, add the background.
  <StepImage src={img2} alt="Hand piping green modeling chocolate around the tiger sketched cookie." />
- Roll black modeling chocolate into thin tubes and add the outline details.
  <StepImage src={img3} alt="Hand using a tool to smooth black modeling chocolate stripes on the cookie." />
- Build up the face using different colored modeling chocolate — use light colors to highlight and dark colors to shadow.
  <StepImage src={img4} alt="Tiger face built with colorful modeling chocolate stripes on a cookie." />
- Use a clay tool to add texture to the fur and details.
  <StepImage src={img5} alt="Hand using a clay tool to add fur texture to the modeling chocolate tiger." />
- Add the nose using black and red modeling chocolate.
  <StepImage src={img6} alt="Hand adding modeling chocolate nose details to the tiger cookie." />
- Add black modeling chocolate to the eye area and use a ball tool to make indentations.
  <StepImage src={img7} alt="Hand using a ball tool to indent eye sockets on the tiger cookie." />
- Roll two eighth-inch balls of yellow modeling chocolate for the eyes. Add a small piece of black for the pupil and a tiny piece of white for the highlight.
  <StepImage src={img8} alt="Hand adjusting yellow modeling chocolate eyes on the tiger face." />
- Use white and royal blue petal dust to add highlights and shadows.
  <StepImage src={img9} alt="Hand dusting petal dust highlights onto the tiger cookie." />
