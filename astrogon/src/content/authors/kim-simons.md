Kim M. Simons is a master cake and food artist. She won Food Network's **Holiday Wars** (2019) and **Cake Wars: Star Wars** (2016), and has competed on numerous other shows including Halloween Wars, SugarDome, and Duff Till Dawn.

Named a Top-Ten Cake Artist in North America by *Dessert Professional* (2013), she is best known for sculpting realistic animals and nature scenes out of sugar. She also authored the gluten-free cookbook *Get All Cook-y With Kim* and painted doves for Sullivan County's Dove Trail honoring the 50th Anniversary of Woodstock.

Kim teaches classes and demos nationwide.
