Snowy Owls Wedding Cake
2012 - Connecticut Cake Competition, Hartford. CT.Show Theme -The Great Outdoors** 1st Place in Wedding Cakes, Master's Division**Grand Prize Winner of Wedding Cake Competition
A wedding cake for a couple who enjoy the great outdoors and share a love for birdwatching. The majestic snowy owl is a rare sighting for birdwatchers. In Native American culture, the owl is a symbol of a new beginning. The snowy owls themselves often mate for life as well.
